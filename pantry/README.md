This fork's own pantry: research that stocks the Menu for Sotto as Diego grows it on `HermeticOrmus/Sotto`, branch `ormus`. Each repo keeps its own pantry, and this one is Sotto's. The format follows the ormus-stack pantry. `TEMPLATE-competitor-map.md`, `TEMPLATE-x-mine.md`, `TEMPLATE-pantry-queue.md` and `TEMPLATE-people-mine.md` are copied verbatim from `Ormus-Solutions/ormus-stack`. The pantry holds a competitor map from public docs, an X mine of praise and complaints with links, an optional people mine of what Sotto's users said in `millZach/Sotto`, and a queue of Goal atoms with done predicates. Nothing here is invented; unknown cells stay `?`.
